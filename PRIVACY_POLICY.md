# Privacy Policy for Joke Book

**Last Updated: June 26, 2025**

Thank you for using Joke Book! This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and managed by the Joke Book application.

### Information We Collect

To provide the core functionality of the app, we collect and store the following information:

1.  **Account Information:** When you create an account using email and password or a third-party service like Google Sign-In, we collect necessary authentication information such as your email address, name, and a unique User ID. This is managed securely by Google Firebase Authentication.

2.  **User-Generated Content:** All content you create within the app, including your jokes, bits, set lists, and audio recordings, is stored in our database (Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage). This content is directly associated with your User ID to enable cloud syncing and access across devices.

3.  **Permissions:**
    *   **Microphone:** We request microphone access for the sole purpose of allowing you to record audio when you explicitly use the recording feature. We do not access the microphone at any other time.

### How We Use Your Information

*   To provide, maintain, and improve the app's functionality.
*   To authenticate your account and allow you to sign in.
*   To securely store and sync your content across your devices.

### Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase as a backend service provider. This includes:
*   Firebase Authentication for user management.
*   Cloud Firestore for database storage.
*   Firebase Storage for audio file storage.

Firebase may collect anonymous usage and analytics data to provide and improve their services. You can review Google's privacy policy for more information.

### Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All data is stored and transmitted using secure services provided by Google Firebase.

By using Joke Book, you consent to our Privacy Policy.
